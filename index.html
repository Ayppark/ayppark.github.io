About

I am a Senior Researcher at the Financial Supervisory Service. I study how networks among financial institutions, firms, investors, and markets shape systemic risk, credit allocation, and market dynamics.

My work combines financial economics with network science, econometrics, and machine learning, with applications to financial stability, supervision, and digital finance.

Research Interests

  • Financial networks and systemic risk Connectedness, contagion, critical transitions, tail risk, and early-warning measurement.
  • Financial intermediation Global banking networks, syndicated lending, loan pricing, and the competition–stability nexus.
  • Computational finance Machine learning, graph methods, natural-language processing, and empirical forecasting.
  • Digital finance and supervision Stablecoins, crypto-asset markets, blockchain networks, AML analytics, and agent-based modeling.
