About Research Publications

About

I am a Senior Researcher at the Financial Supervisory Service. My research combines financial economics, network science, and data-driven methods to study financial stability, bank intermediation, asset markets, and supervisory innovation.

My current work focuses on financial networks, systemic risk, computational finance, and digital-finance supervision. A complete academic and professional record is available in my CV.

Research Interests

  • Financial networks and systemic risk — interconnectedness, contagion, critical transitions, and early-warning indicators.
  • Financial intermediation — global banking networks, syndicated lending, loan pricing, and the competition–stability nexus.
  • Computational finance — machine learning, graph methods, natural-language processing, and empirical forecasting.
  • Digital finance and supervision — crypto-assets, blockchain networks, AML analytics, and agent-based modeling.
