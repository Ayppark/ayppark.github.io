Research Funding

National Research Foundation of Korea (NRF) · July 2021–March 2024

Financial Market Analysis via Connectedness and Deep Learning

KRW 200 million · Five-year grant award

The project examined connectedness among financial institutions and corporate boards, risk propagation, knowledge spillovers, and predictive applications using network and deep-learning methods.

The grant was originally awarded for five years. Project activity concluded in March 2024 following my transition to the Financial Supervisory Service.
