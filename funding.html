Research Funding

National Research Foundation of Korea (NRF)

Financial Market Analysis via Connectedness and Deep Learning

Grant amount
KRW 200 million
Award structure
Five-year research grant
Research period
July 2021 – March 2024

The project examined connectedness among financial institutions and corporate boards, risk propagation, knowledge spillovers, and predictive applications using network and deep-learning methods.

The grant was awarded as a five-year project beginning in July 2021. Research activity continued through March 2024, prior to my transition to the Financial Supervisory Service.
